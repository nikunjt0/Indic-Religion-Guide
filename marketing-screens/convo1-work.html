I feel like I’m working super hard but I’m still behind everyone

The Bhagavad Gita would focus less on whether you’re “ahead” and more on your relationship to the outcome.

Krishna’s karma-yoga teaching is essentially: give yourself fully to the action that is yours to perform without making your peace dependent on the result.

But isn’t that basically saying don’t be ambitious?

Not really. Arjuna is explicitly told to act, not become passive.

There’s a major difference between:

“I want this to succeed.”

and:

“If this doesn’t succeed, I’ve failed as a person.”

The Gita attacks the second much harder than the first.
